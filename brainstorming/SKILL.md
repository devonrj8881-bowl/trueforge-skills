# Brainstorming

1. Restate the goal and confirmed constraints.
2. Generate 3-5 materially different options.
3. Compare complexity, risk, performance, maintainability, and reversibility.
4. Identify assumptions that require evidence.
5. Recommend one option and explain why.
6. Do not edit files until the user approves or explicitly requested implementation.
